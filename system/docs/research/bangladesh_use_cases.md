# Bangladesh problems that real-time Bangla licence plate detection and recognition (ALPR) can address: a fact sheet

Notes compiled 31 Oct 2026. Every number has its year and source. "Snippet" means a search-result summary whose article I did not open; treat those as weaker. Exchange rate is not converted on purpose (1 crore = 10 million; 1 lakh = 100,000).

## 1. Vehicle population and growth, Dhaka congestion cost, road-crash deaths

### Takeaway
Bangladesh had about 6.85 million BRTA-registered motor vehicles by August 2026, roughly three-quarters of them motorcycles, and about 2.4 million were registered in Dhaka. Dhaka is measured as one of the slowest cities in the world, with average speeds of about 4-5 km/h. Published congestion-cost estimates run from Tk 370 billion to over Tk 1 trillion a year. Road deaths in 2025 were 5,490 by BRTA's count and 7,359 by Road Safety Foundation's count. WHO's model estimate is about 31,600 deaths (2021).

### Cited Findings
**Vehicle fleet**
- About 6.85 million vehicles were registered nationwide as of August 2026 (BRTA data), including about 5.1 million motorcycles. Over 2.4 million were registered in Dhaka, about 1.35 million of them motorcycles. About 800 new motorcycles are registered each day nationwide. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Oct 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/f5iiqyoxwt)
- An earlier 2026 report gave a lower count: about 6.6 million registered motor vehicles, of which more than 4.87 million were motorcycles (BRTA data). — [Bonik Barta (2026)](https://en.bonikbarta.com/business/CRly3SEjM0ozuf6V)
- A March 2025 article gave a national total of "about 5.6 million vehicles". This is inconsistent with the 6.6-6.85 million figures above, possibly because of a different cut-off or definition. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Mar 2025](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/3nuoe4u5v8)
- 289,830 motorcycles were registered nationwide in 2025, up 10.3 % from 2024. — [Bonik Barta (2026)](https://en.bonikbarta.com/business/CRly3SEjM0ozuf6V)
- 3.08 lakh (308,000) vehicles of all types were registered in 2024, 14.69 % fewer than in 2023 and a decade low. — [News On AIR, 4 Jan 2025, citing BRTA](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/bangladesh-vehicle-registration-drops-15-to-a-decade-low-in-2024)
- Road Safety Foundation (RSF) says motorcycles make up 71 % of all vehicles nationally. — [Prothom Alo, 10 Jan 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/accident/35tuiqg3oi)
- In Dhaka, seven vehicle types (buses, minibuses, cars, SUVs, microbuses, autorickshaws, motorcycles) totalled 1,921,387 registrations by 2025 (snippet). Bonik Barta cites about 2.24 million registered motor vehicles in Dhaka and notes that many registrations have expired. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/bangladesh/k4p74hlbc3); [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/qrTsh1HLUmFsEzTN)
- Battery-run rickshaws and autorickshaws are largely outside registration. Estimates are 4.5-5 million nationally (Power Development Board) or more than 6 million (BRTA), and 0.6-2 million in Dhaka. A 2026 Innovision Consulting survey found more than 90 % of Dhaka's rickshaw-type vehicles unregistered (snippet). — [Dhaka Tribune (2026)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/408819/battery-run-rickshaws-strain-bangladesh-s-power)
- More than 75,000 buses and trucks have exceeded their economic lifespan. — [Bonik Barta, 21 Jan 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/gyWM582NKumWKeL8/)

**Congestion and speed**
- 2018: BUET's Accident Research Institute (ARI) estimated Dhaka congestion costs Tk 370 billion a year, about 11 % of the national budget, and 5 million working hours a day. — [Financial Express (2018)](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/traffic-congestion-in-dhaka-causes-tk-370b-annual-loss-1526745105)
- 2020: an updated BUET-linked estimate of about Tk 1.53 billion a day (roughly Tk 560 billion a year), with 19 million working hours lost a day (snippet). — [Financial Express (2020)](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/print/dhaka-gridlock-costs-tk-153b-a-day-1608257836)
- 2019: a World Bank figure cited at a seminar put the cost at US$3-5 billion a year (snippet). — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/traffic-gridlock-causes-huge-financial-losses-1608994056)
- 2021: a BIDS conference paper estimated the loss at 2.9 % of GDP, about Tk 1,010 billion on a 2015-16 base (snippet). — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/home/traffic-gridlock-causes-huge-financial-losses-1608994056)
- An MCCI-linked estimate put the cost at Tk 550 billion a year. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/Dhaka-s-traffic-congestion-costs-Tk-550bn-yearly)
- 2023: an NBER study of more than 1,200 cities ranked Dhaka the slowest in the world (speed index -0.60). — [Dhaka Tribune (2023)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/326673/dhaka-traffic); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/709526)
- A BUET study (April 2023) put average speed on Dhaka's main roads at 4.8 km/h; another BUET figure is 4.5 km/h, down from 21 km/h a decade earlier (snippet). Arab News gives a 2024 average of 4.2 km/h. An older World Bank figure is about 7 km/h. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/city/ejgqmk4a0p); [Arab News, 28 Jun 2026](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2648890/world); [Dhaka Tribune (WB)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/dhaka/32349/wb-dhaka%E2%80%99s-average-traffic-speed-7kmph)
- Dhaka has more than 22 million residents. — [Arab News, 28 Jun 2026](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2648890/world)

**Road-crash deaths**
- RSF, 2025: 7,359 deaths, 16,476 injuries and 7,584 road crashes. The 2024 figures were 7,294 deaths, 12,019 injuries and 6,927 crashes. Motorcycle crashes were 3,029 (39.93 % of crashes) and caused 2,672 deaths (36.29 % of deaths). 5,723 of the dead (about 78 %) were aged 18-65. — [Prothom Alo, 10 Jan 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/accident/35tuiqg3oi)
- BRTA, 2025: 5,490 deaths, the highest on BRTA's record. BRTA's annual series rose from 2,635 (2018) to 5,480 (2024). — [Bonik Barta, 21 Jan 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/gyWM582NKumWKeL8/)
- WHO, Global Status Report on Road Safety 2023: an estimated 31,578 deaths in 2021 (18.6 per 100,000), against 5,084 police-reported deaths, about six times fewer. WHO's series for Bangladesh is 21,316 (2015), 24,944 (2018) and 31,578 (2021). — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/381561/who-road-crashes-claiming-young-lives-in)
- Bangladesh Jatri Kalyan Samity reportedly counted 9,111 deaths in 2025 (snippet; year not verified in an opened article). — [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/?p=249140)

### Inferences
- About 74 % of the registered fleet is motorcycles (5.1 M of 6.85 M, Aug 2026). A Bangla ALPR system therefore has to read motorcycle plates well, not only car plates. The front-plate rule (section 3) adds a second, more camera-friendly plate for these vehicles.
- The congestion figures span 2018-2021 and use different methods. For an "impact" argument, quote the range and the year (Tk 370 bn in 2018 to roughly Tk 1 trillion in 2021). Do not present any one of them as current.
- The three death counts differ by up to about 6x, and that gap is itself a finding: Bangladesh lacks a reliable crash-data system. ALPR-linked case records could improve the vehicle-identification part of that data.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain BRTA's official breakdown by vehicle type (cars, buses, trucks, CNG autorickshaws) for 2025-26; BRTA's statistics page was not opened.
- I found no congestion-cost estimate newer than 2021.
- RSF's 2025 economic-loss figure (one snippet said "over Tk 255 billion") could not be tied to an opened article.

## 2. Traffic enforcement today: DMP cases and fines, e-prosecution, CCTV/ITS, existing ANPR

### Takeaway
On 7 May 2026, Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP) launched an AI and ANPR camera system that files cases automatically. The software was built in Bangladesh, with BUET involvement, and the pilot cost about US$50,000. By 30 June 2026 it covered 19 intersections and had produced about 1,500 cases. The binding problems are unreadable, painted or missing plates and millions of unregistered vehicles, which is where a robust Bangla plate reader matters. Earlier signal and ITS projects (World Bank, JICA) largely failed.

### Cited Findings
**The 2026 AI-camera system**
- The "AI-based Road Transport Act 2018 Violation Detection Software" went live on 7 May 2026. Cameras scan plates and auto-generate cases, owners are notified by SMS and registered post, and fines are paid at DMP or through bank or mobile banking. Police said many offenders were not paying; summons or arrest warrants may follow. — [Dhaka Tribune, 7 May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/409621/ai-based-traffic-case-system-goes-live-in-dhaka); [TBS, 3 May 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1428426)
- Ittefaq says the system became operational on 7 April 2026, with enforcement stepped up from 7 May (conflicts with other reports). It recorded nearly 5,000 signal and zebra-crossing violations between 29 April and 16 May, and 478 cases had been filed by 19 May. — [Ittefaq, 19 May 2026](https://en.ittefaq.com.bd/16609/can-ai-cameras-fix-dhaka%E2%80%99s-traffic-mess)
- By 17 May 2026 there were 105 AI-enabled cameras: 25 new ones at six points plus 80 existing PTZ cameras. More than 5,000 violation clips had been collected; about 3,000 had been reviewed and about 1,000 cases were in process. — [Dhaka Tribune, 17 May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/410422/ai-cameras-penalty-points-system-reshape-dhaka)
- About 1,000 cases were generated in the first 10 days. Plates are matched against the BRTA database, and cases are filed against the owner whoever was driving. DMP targets at least 500 camera points citywide within six months. — [Global Voices, 3 Jun 2026](https://globalvoices.org/2026/06/03/ai-cameras-are-surveilling-the-roads-in-dhaka-bangladesh/)
- More than Tk 2.9 million in fines had been collected through AI-camera cases by early June 2026. — [TBS, 6 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1455546)
- 30 Jun 2026: AI cameras were operating at 19 intersections (four added that day). About 1,500 cases had been filed and nearly 38,000 summonses issued. Some notices went to previous owners because ownership had not been transferred after sale. — [TBS, 30 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/ai-run-traffic-cameras-now-19-dhaka-intersections-1500-cases-filed-so-far-dmp-chief)
- The pilot cost about US$50,000, half for software and half for servers, storage and cameras. It uses ANPR and PTZ cameras at seven main intersections, and the software was designed by a BUET team ("entirely homegrown"). Case filing was still manual and the system semi-automatic. Police acknowledged it can wrongly flag drivers who are yielding to pedestrians or autorickshaws. Plans: 60 camera systems in 2026, doubling the next year. — [Arab News, 28 Jun 2026](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2648890/world)
- Detected violations include red-light running, entering a closed left lane, lane violations, wrong-way driving and illegal parking. Police say some plates are unclear or too small to read. Battery rickshaws and vehicles without plates are not in any database, so no cases can be filed against them. Commuters report signal waits falling from 8-12 minutes to 3-4 minutes (anecdotal). BUET ARI's director warned that past upgrades were not maintained. — [TBS, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1470786)
- Plan for 120 major intersections under AI signals and cameras, 76 in the first phase. The signals were developed by BUET, and the pilot started on 1 Sep 2025 at seven junctions. Reported funding sources differ: the police budget or the two city corporations. — [Bonik Barta, 18 Jun 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/pr9vkrHF2gQOeBrq)
- A penalty-points scheme is planned: 12 points per driving licence. Enforcement would shift from the vehicle registration number to the driving licence, nationwide, via a "Road Safety Penalty System" app. BRTA says the point-deduction software is still under development. — [Dhaka Tribune, 17 May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/410422/ai-cameras-penalty-points-system-reshape-dhaka)
- Scam callers impersonate DMP and demand payment for supposed AI-camera cases. — [TBS, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1470786); [Global Voices](https://globalvoices.org/2026/06/03/ai-cameras-are-surveilling-the-roads-in-dhaka-bangladesh/)

**Manual enforcement scale (examples, not annual totals)**
- 22 Sep 2024: 870 cases and Tk 35.79 lakh in fines in one day. Other outlets report 734 or 776 cases for the same day. — [Jago News](https://www.jagonews24.com/en/national/news/77078); [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/359308/dmp-734-cases-filed-31l-fine-imposed-for)
- Around 24 Sep 2025: 1,999 cases in a 24-hour drive (snippet). — [Banglanews24](https://www.banglanews24.com/english/national/news/bd/186305.details)
- Around late April 2025: 3,165 cases over two days (snippet). — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/380061/dmp-files-3-165-cases-against-traffic-rule)

**Past ITS and signal projects**
- World Bank-funded signals at 68 sites (1999-2005) had failed by 2009. Another World Bank-financed effort added 91 intersections (2009-2019), none of which became operational. AI-equipped signals at four intersections, funded by a JICA loan (2014-2018), stopped working within a short time. — [Bonik Barta, 18 Jun 2026](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/pr9vkrHF2gQOeBrq)
- The JICA ITS project (Tk 52 crore; Gulistan/Fulbaria, Paltan, Gulshan-1, Mohakhali) received four extensions and never came fully into operation. Headline: "Tk 108 crore squandered on traffic lights since 2001." — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/dhaka/315408/108c-squandered-on-traffic-lights-since-2001); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/supplement/ineffective-tech-fixes-dhaka-traffic-just-waste-funds-1059451)

### Inferences
- Bangladesh already has a live ANPR deployment, so the team's work fits a real government pipeline. It is small: about US$50k, 7-19 intersections, and roughly 1,500 cases in eight weeks. The stated targets are 60 to 500 camera points and 120 intersections, so throughput, cost per camera and reading accuracy will matter as it scales.
- A plate reader that handles small, unclear and motorcycle plates directly addresses the failure modes officials named. Reading accuracy also decides whether a case goes to the right owner, and the scam calls and wrong-owner notices show the trust cost of errors.
- Annual DMP case and fine totals were not found. The daily examples (about 700-2,000 cases a day in drives) suggest manual enforcement on the order of hundreds of thousands of cases a year. That is an extrapolation, not a reported figure.

### Gaps
- No full-year DMP figures for traffic cases or fines (2024, 2025) were found. A snippet mentioned about 100,000 unpaid traffic cases pending since 2022, but I could not verify it in an opened article.
- No reported ANPR read-accuracy figure for the DMP system. Claims that rain, night and congestion hurt plate reading appeared only in a search summary and were not confirmed in the articles I opened.
- No AI-camera case totals after 30 Jun 2026.
- No data on ALPR outside Dhaka (Chattogram, highways).

## 3. BRTA retro-reflective plates and RFID, fake or unregistered vehicles, theft

### Takeaway
Retro-reflective plates with RFID tags were introduced in 2012, but in practice the RFID layer has failed: about 90 % of RFID stickers do not read at RHD toll booths, and DMP said in May 2026 that many tags are inactive. In 2025 about 78,000 vehicles were reported to be running on fake plates. Painted plates, uncollected plates and millions of unregistered battery rickshaws remain. In 2026 BRTA ordered motorcycle front plates, owner-paid and without RFID, by 15 Nov 2026, and trialled them with AI cameras. In effect the state is moving from RFID to camera-based plate reading.

### Cited Findings
- Digital plates and RFID tags were introduced in 2012, and BRTA said RFID would identify vehicles "under any circumstances". It "has not fully delivered", and transport expert Shamsul Haque said the government must first establish why the tags failed. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Oct 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/f5iiqyoxwt)
- Plates are made by Bangladesh Machine Tools Factory in Gazipur. The 2012 project introduced standardised retro-reflective plates with embossed characters and an RFID tag. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bangladesh)
- RFID stickers fail in about 90 % of cases at ETC booths, which cannot read them automatically. At Jamuna Bridge, RFID tags are detected automatically for only 15 % of vehicles. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Mar 2025](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/3nuoe4u5v8)
- 11 May 2026: a DMP notice said many vehicles run without BRTA-issued plates and RFID tags, that tags are often inactive "creating complications for city traffic management", and that legal action would start the following week. — [The Daily Star, 11 May 2026](https://thedailystar.net/news/governance/news/action-against-vehicles-without-brta-issued-number-plates-rfid-tags-dmp-4173456)
- Known problems: some vehicles use painted numbers instead of official plates; some owners paid for RFID plates but never collected them; installed RFID tags often fail; and unclear or missing plates make identification hard. — [Dhaka Tribune, 17 May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/410422/ai-cameras-penalty-points-system-reshape-dhaka)
- In an earlier year, BRTA tried to clear a backlog of about 5 lakh (500,000) plates and tags waiting to be installed (Daily Star, older article, date not confirmed). — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/taxpayers-get-no-service-42789)
- Fake plates: a Daily Ittefaq report (25 May 2025) said 78,000 vehicles were running with fake plates. The BRTA chairman said a list had gone to DMP and Highway Police, and that documents for nearly 600,000 vehicles were yet to be updated. — [Daily Ittefaq op-ed, 26 May 2025](https://en.ittefaq.com.bd/12055/how-do-vehicles-run-with-fake-number-plates)
- Motorcycle front-plate rule: a BRTA notice (16 Oct 2026) requires front plates by 15 Nov 2026, paid for by owners. They are conventional plates, not RFID: stainless steel or aluminium, white with black Bangla script, characters at least 20 mm high. — [bdnews24, 16 Oct 2026](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/trrte0u6fs)
- Prothom Alo gives sizes of 200 x 100 mm for scooters and 285 x 50 mm for other motorcycles; bdnews24 gives 285 x 50 mm for scooters (conflict). The decision came from a pollution-control meeting chaired by the PM on 18 July 2026. A seven-member BRTA committee was formed on 13 Aug, and sample plates were tested with AI cameras on 19 Aug and 5 Sep 2026 on Lake Road near Parliament. The plate fee is Tk 2,260, so fitting all 5.1 M motorcycles at that price would exceed Tk 11.52 billion. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Oct 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/f5iiqyoxwt)
- Wikipedia states the front-plate rule's purpose is ALPR camera surveillance (not confirmed by the bdnews24 article). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bangladesh)
- Vehicles without fitness certificates: BRTA once told the High Court that more than 4.58 lakh unfit vehicles were on the road, 1.68 lakh of them in Dhaka. TBS reported 4,79,320 vehicles running without fitness certificates. Both are older and their dates are unconfirmed (snippets). The government lost Tk 407.57 million in FY2024-25 from vehicles lacking fitness renewal. — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/3763); [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/business/9y1XHN7VbADvqwBU)
- Theft: DMP recovered 1,123 stolen vehicles between January 2019 and July 2021, 545 of them motorcycles. It filed 929 cases, mostly bike theft, and arrested 478 people. Victims reported poor police cooperation and delays in filing cases. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/crime-and-law/stolen-bike-network-all-over-the-country)
- Around Sep 2025, Detective Branch police seized 13 vehicles (nine private cars, a microbus and three motorbikes) from a carjacking gang (snippet). Separate DB cases involved cars bearing fake plates (snippet, undated). — [Banglanews24](https://www.banglanews24.com/english/national/news/bd/8520.details); [Banglanews24](https://www.banglanews24.com/national/article/71039/DB-police-bust-vehicle-theft-gang-in-city)

### Inferences
- RFID was meant to be the national identifier, but it does not read reliably (about 90 % failure at toll booths). The plate itself, read by camera, is therefore the practical identifier, and the 2026 front-plate mandate and AI-camera trials support that.
- ALPR plus a BRTA database lookup can flag fake plates (number not registered, or registered to a different vehicle class or colour), duplicated plates (the same number seen at two places at once), stolen vehicles (hotlist match) and vehicles with expired fitness or tax tokens. These are the problems the BRTA chairman and DMP describe. This depends on database access, which is an institutional issue, not a model issue.
- Painted and non-standard plates are a real-world input distribution. A model trained only on clean BMTF retro-reflective plates will underperform on Dhaka streets.

### Gaps
- No official count of vehicles fitted with retro-reflective/RFID plates versus those still without was found.
- No national stolen-vehicle statistics for 2023-2026 were found. Police HQ crime tables do not appear to break out vehicle theft in the sources found.
- The 78,000 fake-plate figure comes from a single newspaper report (May 2025); the original Ittefaq news report was not opened.

## 4. Tolls: Padma Bridge, Jamuna Bridge, Dhaka Elevated Expressway, Dhaka-Bhanga (Mawa) expressway, Karnaphuli tunnel

### Takeaway
Toll volumes are large: Padma Bridge carried 26.9 million vehicles and collected Tk 3,429 crore up to June 2026. Electronic tolling is marginal: about 6 % of RHD toll traffic in February 2025, and roughly 1 % of Padma crossings in the first ten months of ETC. RFID failure, low registration and separate software systems are the reasons. A manual booth handles only 300-400 vehicles an hour, and queues of "miles" form at Eid. Large shares of toll revenue go to toll-operating contractors.

### Cited Findings
**Padma Bridge**
- Three years to June 2025: Tk 2,504.67 crore from 19,471,692 vehicles (Tk 798.61 crore in year 1, 850.44 in year 2, 861.22 in year 3). — [Dhaka Tribune (Jun 2025)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/nation/384852/padma-bridge-marks-3yrs-of-operation-over-2-500c)
- To 29 Jun 2026: 26,862,808 vehicles and Tk 3,429.45 crore (Bridge Authority). — [Ittefaq, 1 Jul 2026](https://en.ittefaq.com.bd/17181/padma-bridge-toll-revenue-reaches-tk-3-429cr)
- Average traffic is about 16,000 vehicles a day, roughly doubling at Eid. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Mar 2025](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/3nuoe4u5v8)
- Non-stop ETC live pilot from 15 Sep 2025. Users verify a BRTA-approved RFID tag once at the Mawa booth and pay by mobile wallet or bank top-up. — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/live-piloting-of-etc-for-padma-bridge-to-begin-september-15); [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/8ckvm0cvrk)
- By 27 Jul 2026: Tk 10,08,24,600 (about Tk 10.08 crore) collected via ETC, 1,008 vehicles registered and 56,406 ETC crossings since launch, at about 60 km/h. — [UNB, 27 Jul 2026](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/padma-bridge-etc-toll-revenue-surpasses-tk-10-crore-vehicle-registrations-top-1000/191657)

**Jamuna Bridge and RHD network**
- 26 May 2026: Padma had over 800 ETC registrations and Jamuna 6,774. ETC on the two bridges handled 448,650 vehicles and Tk 604 million in tolls since 15 September. Registrations are low partly because of "separate software systems". RHD runs over 71 toll bridges, only 17 of them with ETC (one lane each), and had 62,749 ETC registrations. In 2025, about 2.9 million ETC vehicles used 15 RHD bridges, paying about Tk 600 million. — [Financial Express, 26 May 2026](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/print/etc-reg-surges-ahead-of-eid-1779729204)
- 8 Feb 2025: over 127,000 vehicles crossed RHD's eight major bridges and two toll roads, and only 7,687 (about 6 %) used ETC. Jamuna carries about 22,000 vehicles a day on average, over 50,000 at Eid. A manual booth handles at most 300-400 vehicles an hour, and BUET professor Md Hadiuzzaman says automation could raise that fourfold. Revenue: RHD about Tk 12 billion a year; the four Bridges Division bridges plus the tunnel over Tk 17 billion a year. ETC failure reasons: banks and mobile wallets not integrated, RFID stickers not working, low prepaid balances. — [Prothom Alo, 25 Mar 2025](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/3nuoe4u5v8)

**Contractors and leakage**
- Dhaka-Bhanga (Mawa) expressway: tolls since 1 Jul 2022 run by Korea Expressway Corporation under a contract of about Tk 960 million a year (Tk 7.17 billion over five years) awarded without a tender. About 80 % of toll revenue has gone to the contractor, and tolls collected to Sep 2023 totalled Tk 3.88 billion. — [Prothom Alo, 13 Nov 2024](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/992xo4qdpg)
- Padma toll and maintenance contract: Tk 6.93 billion over five years, about 15 % of the Tk 800 million monthly toll. An inquiry reportedly confirmed inflated toll-collector salary claims, but no action followed. Karnaphuli tunnel operator CCCC: Tk 6.84 billion over five years, with a monthly deficit of about Tk 95 million (late 2024). — [Prothom Alo, 13 Nov 2024](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/992xo4qdpg)

**Karnaphuli (Bangabandhu) tunnel**
- IMED (30 Jun 2026): actual traffic is about 4,000-4,500 vehicles a day against a 2025 forecast of 28,350 (about 14 %). The toll is Tk 100, earning about Tk 12 lakh a day against Tk 22 lakh a day in operations and maintenance, a deficit of about Tk 36.5 crore a year. — [TBS, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/infrastructure/tk10689cr-karnaphuli-tunnel-struggling-close-revenue-gap-amid-thin-traffic)

**Dhaka Elevated Expressway (PPP, operator FDEE)**
- Opened Sep 2023. In February 2024 it averaged about 42,000 vehicles and Tk 3 million a day; the operator needs about 50,000 a day for its target return; design capacity is about 80,000 a day. — [Prothom Alo (2024)](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/city/8pb53u24vq); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/695718)
- The car toll is Tk 80. — [TBS (2023)](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/712226)
- The full design has 11 toll plazas and 43 booths with automated tolling. — [BUET ASCE student chapter](https://ascesc.ce.buet.ac.bd/2024/12/13/dhaka-elevated-expressway)

### Inferences
- Padma ETC share (calculated): Padma carried about 7.39 million vehicles between late June 2025 and 29 Jun 2026, about 20,000 a day. Against that, 56,406 ETC crossings in about 10 months is roughly 1 % of traffic.
- Average Padma toll per vehicle is about Tk 1,277 (calculated: Tk 3,429.45 crore / 26.86 M).
- Where RFID fails (about 90 % at RHD booths), a camera ALPR could act as a backup reader: match the plate to the registered ETC account, or flag a vehicle that passes without paying. Plate-based video tolling would also give an independent audit count of vehicles, useful where large toll shares go to contractors and inflated costs are alleged. These are proposed uses, not reported deployments.
- The Karnaphuli tunnel's thin traffic (about 4,000 a day) makes it a low-risk pilot site for plate-based tolling.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 daily traffic or ETC status for the Dhaka Elevated Expressway. A snippet said the Bridge Authority asked FDEE to install ETC to cut peak-hour queues; the date and outcome are not verified.
- No measured toll-plaza queue times (minutes) were found, only descriptions of "miles" of jams at Eid.
- No audit putting a figure on toll leakage was found.

## 5. Parking, fuel stations, land ports, Chattogram port, campuses and factories, fleets, ride-sharing

### Takeaway
The 2026 fuel crisis created a nationwide per-vehicle rationing rule (for example 2 litres a day for motorcycles). It was enforced with paper receipts and, for ride-share bikes, by matching the registration number against the app: plate-based identity checks done by hand. Chattogram port handles about 8,000 trucks a day and already runs a digital gate pass. Parking, land-port and ride-share problems are real but mostly documented with older data.

### Cited Findings
**Fuel rationing (2026)**
- BPC directive of 6 Mar 2026 (Middle East war, Hormuz closure, about 95 % of fuel imported). Daily caps: motorcycles 2 L petrol or octane; cars 10 L; SUVs and microbuses 20-25 L; pickups and local buses 70-80 L diesel; long-distance buses and trucks 200-220 L. Enforcement: buyers must show the previous receipt to buy again. Queues stretched for blocks in Dhaka; one Uber driver waited about 50 minutes; motorcyclists fought over queue places. — [Prothom Alo, 6 Mar 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/u2rd23k06t)
- 10 Mar 2026: ride-share motorcycles in cities may buy 5 L a day, but only after the motorcycle's registration number and the driver's details are matched against the ride-sharing app; other motorcycles stay at 2 L. — [Prothom Alo, 10 Mar 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/wzm1k9w3pe); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/ride-share-bikers-allowed-5-litres-daily-what-about-regular-riders-1382516)
- 28 Mar 2026, Chattogram: stations set money caps (for example Tk 500 for motorcycles, Tk 1,000-2,000 for cars). "Tag officers" were deployed; customers waited over 30 minutes; hoarding was alleged. — [TBS, 28 Mar 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/fuel-rationing-underway-ctg-filling-stations-after-deployment-tag-officers-1396086)
- A man was reportedly killed in Jhenaidah after an altercation with filling-station staff over refuelling (snippet). — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2635722/world)

**Chattogram seaport**
- About 8,000 vehicles move through the port each day. An online gate pass (18 Feb 2025) cut entry time from 15-20 minutes to 1-2 minutes. Nearly 100,000 truck drivers and helpers are registered with photos. — [TBS, 18 Feb 2025](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1071451)
- In Oct 2025 a gate-fee hike led prime-mover and trailer operators to halt container transport; it resumed after the CPA reverted the fee. — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/container-transport-ctg-port-resumes-4013861); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1261726)

**Benapole land port**
- At various times, queues of 5,000-10,000 trucks have formed at Petrapole/Benapole, with waits of 30-50 days reported at peaks. One report says crossing time fell to 3 days and the queue from about 9,000 to 1,587 trucks. All of these are older or undated. — [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/5000-trucks-waiting-to-enter-bangladesh-through-benapole); [Financial Express](https://old.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/6000-benapole-bound-trucks-stranded-at-petrapole-in-india-1635825572); [Maritime Gateway](https://www.maritimegateway.com/from-petrapole-to-benapole-in-3-days/)

**Parking**
- Dhaka's 330,968 private cars (BRTA, July 2019) would need about 8 crore (80 million) sq ft of parking. — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/city/news/8-crore-sqft-needed-just-cars-study-1878253)
- A 2014 RAJUK study found that RAJUK and city-corporation off-street facilities held only 1,474 vehicles. — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/127741/street-parking-making-the-illegal-legal)
- Illegal parking is one of the violations the 2026 AI cameras target. — [TBS, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1470786)

**Ride-sharing**
- BRTA rules: a vehicle may be used for ride-sharing only after one year of registration, and only if personally owned; some operators broke this. Street-hailed (off-app) motorcycle rides leave passengers without a driver or registration record. In 2020 BRTA allowed only 255 enlisted vehicles to resume ride-sharing. — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/ridesharing-companies-breach-registration-rules-1546763800); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/632642); [The Daily Star (2020)](https://www.thedailystar.net/ride-sharing-services-brta-permits-only-255-enlisted-vehicles-1918061)

### Inferences
- In 2026 the government had no per-vehicle fuel identity system and fell back on paper receipts, which a buyer can get around by going to another station. An ALPR log at pump entry, shared between stations, is a direct technical fit for this kind of per-vehicle cap. It would also have automated the ride-share registration-number check that pumps did by hand. This is a proposal, not a reported deployment.
- At Chattogram port, about 8,000 trucks a day already pass a digital gate pass. Plate reading at the gate could tie the pass to the actual truck and catch passes being reused.
- Parking enforcement, campus and factory gates, and fleet yards are standard commercial ALPR uses. For Bangladesh I found no market-size or incident data, so argue these as qualitative opportunities.

### Gaps
- No 2023-2026 data were found for Benapole queue lengths, gated communities, universities, garment factories, fleet or logistics companies, or crime linked to ride-sharing.
- A snippet claimed Dhaka had "4.3 million registered bike riders" (January 2024). That conflicts with about 1.35 M registered motorcycles in Dhaka (August 2026) and looks unreliable, so I excluded it.
- The end date of the 2026 fuel rationing was not confirmed.

## 6. Bangla plate format and local OCR difficulties

### Takeaway
Bangladeshi plates normally have two lines in Bangla script: the city or district word (plus "Metro" for big cities) and a class letter on top, and a 2-digit series and 4-digit number below. Plate colour encodes use. Difficulties include about 100 or more glyph classes, two-row layouts, non-standard and painted plates, old plates without a series number, and the new single-line front plates on motorcycles.

### Cited Findings
- Format: "city - vehicle class letter and number - vehicle number" (for example DHAKA-D-11-9999), in Bengali letters and numerals. The series runs 11-99 and the number 0001-9999. Older vehicles may show only a 1-4 digit number with no series. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bangladesh)
- Class letters (partial; the Wikipedia table is partly garbled):
  - Motorcycles: অ up to 100 cc, হ 101-125 cc, ল 126-165 cc.
  - Cars: ক up to 1000 cc, খ 1001-1300 cc, গ 1301-2000 cc, ভ above 2000 cc.
  - Other passenger vehicles: ঘ SUVs, চ microbuses, ছ human haulers, প taxis, জ minibuses, ঝ coaches, ব inter-district buses, স school buses, থ/ত CNG autorickshaws.
  - Goods and special vehicles: ম delivery vans, ন/ট/ড/উ trucks, ঢ tankers and articulated vehicles, শ special-purpose, ই agricultural.
  - Government offices: য PM's Office, র President's Office.
  — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bangladesh)
- Colours: private plates are white with black characters; commercial (hire) plates green with black; EVs green with white; diplomatic yellow or orange; military, police and navy black or blue. Before 2012, plates were sometimes hand-painted with non-uniform colours. Sizes: car 300 x 152 mm, bus or truck 350 x 152 mm, motorcycle rear 205 x 152 mm. Fancy numbers have been sold since June 2020. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bangladesh)
- New motorcycle front plates: 285 x 50 mm (scooters 200 x 100 mm per Prothom Alo), white background, black Bangla script, characters at least 20 mm high, no decoration. — [bdnews24, 16 Oct 2026](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/trrte0u6fs); [Prothom Alo, 25 Oct 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/f5iiqyoxwt)
- Research notes: no public dataset (as of 2021), and the script needs more than 100 unique classes (words, letters, digits) (Shomee et al., DICTA 2021). OCR-based approaches often perform poorly on Bangla plates because of complex layouts, font variation and mixed character sets (Chakroborty, 2026). Bangla's complex character structure makes plates hard (Hasin, arXiv 2603.10267). — [arXiv 2109.00906](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2109.00906); [arXiv 2603.10267](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.10267)
- Field problems reported by DMP: painted numbers, unclear or small plates, missing plates, and vehicles without plates (battery rickshaws). — [Dhaka Tribune, 17 May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/410422/ai-cameras-penalty-points-system-reshape-dhaka); [TBS, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1470786)

### Inferences
- A 285 x 50 mm front plate with characters at least 20 mm high is likely a single-line layout, unlike the two-line rear plate. A deployed reader will need to handle both, and a model trained only on two-row plates may fail on the new front plates. This is inferred from the dimensions; I found no official layout drawing.
- The class letter carries meaning (engine size or vehicle type), so plate-versus-vehicle mismatches (for example a truck-class letter on a car) are a cheap fake-plate check once OCR is reliable.
- The plate grammar (district word from a closed list of about 64 districts plus metro cities, one class letter from a closed list, 2-digit series of 11-99, 4-digit number) supports a lexicon or format-constrained decoder. This is a known way to raise exact-match accuracy.

### Gaps
- No official BRTA specification of the font or of the full class-letter list was found (the Wikipedia table is partly garbled).
- No published field accuracy for DMP's ANPR on Bangla plates, so the team's roughly 90 % plate-exact result cannot be compared with the deployed system on public evidence.
