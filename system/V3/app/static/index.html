How to use

  1. Read a photo: drop or choose a car photo. The plate is found, cut out and read.
  2. Check the result: the Bangla text is shown big, with its English form and a status.
  3. It is saved: the plate picture goes to records/plate_images/ and a row to records/plates.csv.
  4. Saved records: search, filter, and press Export to get a zip with the CSV, the plate pictures and a printable sheet.

What the status means

  • Verified the readers agree and the plate has a valid BRTA layout.
  • Likely a valid layout, but the readers did not fully agree.
  • Check manually the text may be wrong: look at the plate picture.

Night mode

Auto (the default) turns it on from 6 pm to 6 am by this device's clock; On / Off force it. At night every reader also reads a contrast-boosted copy of the plate and all readings vote. On the live camera it switches the phone light on where the browser allows it. A dark photo read in the daytime gets a Read again in night mode button. Each record's Night mode column says how it was read.

Measured on the held-out test set (22 Nov 2026): night plate crops 84.6 % → 86.2 % right, whole night photos 83.3 % → 83.3 % (no change), about 3× the reading time. It is a small help, not a fix: better night results need night training data and light on the plate (phone light or an IR lamp).

Open the CSV in Excel
